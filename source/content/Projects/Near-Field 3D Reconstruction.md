**Near-Field 3D Reconstruction** — dense 3D reconstruction and geometry-aware perception for objects seen up close.

**Stack:** PyTorch · 3D computer vision · differentiable rendering · geometry processing

**Problem:** objects in near-field views are heavily truncated and self-occluded — standard reconstruction pipelines trained on full-object views fail. Need a setup robust to close-range, partial observations.
