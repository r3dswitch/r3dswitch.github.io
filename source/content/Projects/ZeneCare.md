**ZeneCare — AI-Guided Chronic Wound Care**

An AI pipeline that turns smartphone images of chronic wounds into clinically useful measurements and predictions.

**Pipeline:**

```text
Smartphone images
        ↓
Segmentation
        ↓
Tissue classification
        ↓
3D measurements
        ↓
Healing / risk prediction
        ↓
Clinical recommendations
```
